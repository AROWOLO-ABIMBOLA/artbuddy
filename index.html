    Meet the maker

    Dr. Arowolo Ayoola

    Dr. Arowolo Ayoola is a mathematician. He loves numbers, and he also loves colours, shapes and patterns, because patterns are everywhere in maths and in art!

    He started a company called Data-Lead Africa. His team counts things that really matter, and then uses maths to find clever ways to make everyday life better for families.

    He made Art Buddy so that you can paint, draw and make beautiful things, and learn about colours and the artists of Nigeria and the world. Who knows? One day you might make art that people everywhere love!
